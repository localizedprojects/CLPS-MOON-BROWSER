Lunar Horizon privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. This policy covers the Lunar Horizon app for Android and iOS and the web app at localizedprojects.github.io/CLPS-MOON-BROWSER.

The short version

Lunar Horizon does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

The app remembers three small settings on your device so it opens the way you left it: the planning goal you picked, whether the time controls are hidden, and whether you have already seen the short tutorial (so it only shows on the first launch). They stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app (on the web, when you clear the site's data).

Network use

  • The Moon data, maps and charts are built into the app, so the Android and iOS apps work fully offline. The Android app does not even ask for internet access.
  • The web app is loaded from GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses); see GitHub's privacy statement. The web app itself makes no other requests.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no location, camera, microphone or contacts, and no access to your photos (iOS asks only if you choose to save an exported chart to Photos).

Files you export

When you choose to save or share a chart or a CSV table, the app hands the file to your device's share sheet (or your browser's download). Where it goes next is up to you and the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

On iPhone and iPad, a chart is saved to your Photos only if you pick “Save Image” in that share sheet; iOS then asks you for permission to add it. The app never reads your photo library.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
